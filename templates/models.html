Evaluation Metrics in Precision Agriculture

Why each metric matters in this engineering domain.

Accuracy

Measures total correct predictions (both irrigation and non-irrigation) over all test samples. High overall accuracy ensures baseline reliability.

Precision

When the model predicts "Irrigate", how often is water actually needed? High precision eliminates false alarms and saves water pump electricity.

Recall (Sensitivity)

Out of all cases where the field genuinely needed water, how many did the system catch? Crucial to avoid crop wilting caused by false negatives.

F1-Score

The harmonic mean of Precision and Recall. Our system automatically ranks and chooses the best algorithm based on the highest F1-Score for optimal balance.
